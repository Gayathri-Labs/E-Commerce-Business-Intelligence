-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Overall Business Performance

	The business generated $23.16M in total sales and $2.61M in total profit from 113,270 orders. 
	A total of 42,047 unique customers placed orders during the analysis period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 2. Average Sales Value

	Each order generated an average sales value of $204.50. This indicates a consistent spending pattern across customers.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 3. Overall Profit Margin

	The overall profit margin was 11.28%. This shows that the business maintained healthy profitability from its total sales.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 4. Profitable vs Loss Making Orders

	Around 81% of orders were profitable, while only 19% resulted in a loss. Most customer orders contributed positively to the business.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 5. Monthly Sales Trend

	Monthly sales remained stable throughout the year. Revenue consistently stayed between $820K and $1.1M, 
	showing steady business performance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 6. Highest Sales Month

	October 2022 recorded the highest sales revenue. Seasonal demand may have contributed to this strong performance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 7. Category Performance

	Office Supplies generated the highest sales, while Technology contributed the lowest sales.
	 Office Supplies was the strongest performing category overall.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 8. Best Selling Products

	Products such as Staples, Staple envelopes, and Easy staple paper generated the highest sales revenue. 
	These products were consistently popular among customers.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 9. Most Profitable Products

	The same top selling products also generated the highest profit.
	 This indicates that they performed well in both sales volume and profitability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 10. Top Customers by Sales

	The top 10 customers each spent close to $2,000.
 	Although no single customer dominated sales, these customers contributed consistently.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 11. Top Customers by Profit

	Imani Webber generated the highest profit, followed by Nelle Brown. These customers were among the most valuable to the business.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 12. Customer Segment Performance

	The Consumer segment generated the highest sales and profit. It was the most important customer group for the business.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 13. Orders by Customer Segment

	The Consumer segment placed the highest number of orders. This indicates strong customer engagement from individual buyers.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 14. Regional Performance

	The West region achieved the highest sales and profit, while the South recorded the lowest sales. 
	The West was the strongest performing region.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 15. State Performance

	California generated the highest sales revenue, followed by New York and Texas. 
	These states were the major contributors to overall business revenue.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 16. City Performance

	New York City contributed the highest sales, followed by Los Angeles and Philadelphia. 
	Large metropolitan cities played a key role in business growth.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 17. Average Order Value by Region

	The Central region recorded the highest average order value. However, the average order values across all regions were very similar.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 18. Delivery Status Distribution

	More than half of all orders were delivered late. This highlights an opportunity to improve delivery performance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 19. Most Used Shipping Type

	Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping method. It handled the majority of customer orders.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 20. Shipping Type Performance

	Standard Class generated the highest sales and profit. It was the most effective shipping method for overall business performance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 21. Average Delivery Delay

	Second Class experienced the highest average delivery delay. In contrast, Standard Class showed the best delivery performance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 22. Delivery Performance

	Late deliveries were significantly higher than early and on time deliveries. Reducing delivery delays could improve customer satisfaction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 23. Profit by Category

	Office Supplies generated the highest profit. Although Technology had the lowest profit, it still remained profitable overall.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 24. Monthly Profit Trend

	October 2022 recorded the highest monthly profit, while January 2021 had the lowest. Profitability improved during peak sales periods.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 25. Sales and Profit by Weekday

	Saturday generated the highest sales, while Monday recorded the highest profit.
	 Wednesday showed the weakest business performance compared to other weekdays.
